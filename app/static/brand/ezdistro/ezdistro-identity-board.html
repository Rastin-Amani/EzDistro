What we heard

The brief

An SEO automation platform that turns a WordPress site into a self-feeding content engine: it indexes existing posts, plans and writes new articles, and publishes them back. Audience: SEO teams and content agencies. The mark should feel like effortless control — calm, precise, quietly engineered.

calmpreciseeffortlessengineered

Success: reads at 16 px and on signage, one-colour capable, anchored in distribution (not AI or SEO clichés), and quiet enough to sit beside the product UI.

EzDistro — final identity2
Final design

e-Loop

A lowercase e that never stops — the self-feeding content loop.

  • The e is the brand initial and the idea: a loop that returns enriched
  • One continuous stroke — a single source, always cycling
  • One electric blue — the mark never needs a second colour
  • A single bar through the loop's core is the feed line
  • Reads at 16 px and in one colour
